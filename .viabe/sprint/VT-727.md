# VT-727 — ingest the whole corpus

## Why this row is worded this way
Fazal chose the staged path (VT-726 seed → prove → **then this**) and attached an explicit
instruction that the full ingestion **must not be missed**. He has seen the failure mode before:
the pipe goes green on a small seed, the demo works, and the corpus quietly never lands. This row
exists so that outcome requires someone to actively close a Critical row, not merely to forget.

**This row does not close because VT-726 proved the pipe. It closes when the corpus is in.**

## Scope
Run the full VT-710 ingestion over `archives/business-knowledge/` (118 cards across the
compliance + executional trees, per MANIFEST):
- Rights/originality gate per **CL-2026-07-29b-knowledge-not-source**: inclusion turns on the
  **accuracy and value** of the knowledge and the originality of OUR expression — **not** the
  source's licence. Verbatim-expression detection blocks; unknown licence does not. Paywall
  circumvention stays excluded outright. ToS/concentration remain non-blocking flags.
- Deterministic pipeline-assigned metadata: source_class (T1/T1v/T2/T3/T4), claim_key
  normalization, independence clusters (N retellings of one study = ONE corroboration),
  applicability (jurisdiction / size / industry / maturity / channel / effective period —
  regulatory cards REQUIRE effective dates), `domain` (VT-726's column).
- Admission as `candidate`; promotion to `validated` follows the deterministic auto-checks.
  **No card advises a tenant on volume alone** — the graduation bars are D7 and Fazal's.
- Persisted card embeddings (VT-726's in-process shadow embed does not scale to 118 and does not
  survive a restart). Migration number via the allocator — never hand-picked (CL-424).
- Concentration report: largest-single-source share, flagged not blocking (was 4.24% at the
  rights reset; recheck at full scale).

## Exit gate
- All 118 files processed with a per-file verdict: ingested / rejected / deferred, each with a
  reason. **A silent drop is a failure of this row**, not an acceptable outcome.
- Registry counts reconcile against the manifest — stated, not assumed.
- Global-purity tests re-run at full scale: no tenant identifier in any global text field.
- Independence clustering demonstrably collapses at least one multi-retelling group, or an
  explicit statement that no such group exists in the corpus.
- Retrieval over the full registry returns ranked, cluster-deduped candidates within the
  declared per-identity token budgets — the diversity rule (max 1–2 per cluster) proven, not
  assumed.
