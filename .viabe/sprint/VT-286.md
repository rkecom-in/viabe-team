# VT-286 — WhatsApp WABA onboarding via Meta Embedded Signup

## RE-FILING NOTE (Fazal 2026-08-10) — this is an UPGRADE, not a gate

**Fazal, verbatim:** *"Having owner-owned WABA is a dependency that we won't be able to manage to
meet as it has the owner to take certain actions, especially the WABA onboarding, and several other
configuration, template whitelisting, approvals, etc. Unless these entire thing can be automated
without the owners involvement, these will just act as blockers."*

**He is applying CL-421** (zero-manual-paste connectors after OAuth, locked after the Apps Script
paste proved customer-hostile to the Tier-2/3 SMB persona). Embedded Signup is heavier than a paste
step, so requiring it at signup contradicts a decision already made.

**The specific wall, beyond the paperwork:** WABA onboarding needs a number **not already on
consumer WhatsApp**. Our persona runs their business from the WhatsApp app on that very phone;
migrating it to the API means losing the app on it. Buying a second SIM for an unproven service is a
non-starter at signup. Add Meta business verification (documents, days-to-weeks, frequent
rejections) and a Business Manager account many owners lack.

**New position in the journey:** the friction is unacceptable at signup and perfectly acceptable at
month three, when the owner has seen revenue and has a reason to do paperwork. So VT-286 becomes:
- an **opt-in upgrade** offered to tenants who have earned it, and
- a **bring-your-own-WABA** path for larger tenants who already have one.

Same code, opposite position in the journey.

**What we accept by deferring it, stated plainly:** every tenant shares our sender reputation, so
one tenant blasting a stale list degrades deliverability for all. That risk is managed by **VT-742**
(sender resolution, pinning, per-tenant quality accounting, throttle, block capture, aggregate caps)
and **VT-743** (prevention at source) — all of which require **zero owner action**, which is the
constraint Fazal set. The trade is right at our size and genuinely dangerous at 500 tenants.

**Correction of record:** Clau twice told Fazal this row was "rostered and unbuilt." It is built —
`integrations/whatsapp_account.py`, `api/whatsapp_oauth.py`, `apps/team-web/lib/onboard/connect.ts`.
The recommendation survived the error (deferring it was right either way), but the error was the
fifth stale-status claim in two days and was made in the same conversation that introduced
provenance tags to prevent exactly it.

## Why
Customer comms run on the **owner's own WABA** — Meta mandates client-owned WhatsApp
Business Accounts; On-Behalf-Of is dead. Business-forward identity, Viabe disclosed
as operator/processor. This is the launch-critical entry point: without an owner WABA
connected + verified, no customer messaging happens. Inbound-first model (VT-287)
builds on top. Zero manual paste after the owner approves (CL-421).

## What
Owner-owned WABA connect via Meta **Embedded Signup** (Twilio tech-provider):
- `build_embedded_signup_url(tenant, ...)` → Meta popup (owner's ~5-min flow:
  Embedded Signup + business verification + display-name = their shop name + privacy
  URL).
- callback (uses the **VT-289 hardened state** nonce; tenant from stored record) →
  token exchange → provision a **dedicated NEW number per tenant** (Fazal's choice) →
  submit display name → persist tokens encrypted per-tenant (reuse the
  `tenant_oauth_tokens` pattern; Fernet `encrypt_value`).
- System user automates all post-grant config (no further owner steps).
- Track per-tenant status: `pending → verifying → name-approved → live`. A tenant
  CANNOT send until `live` (Jan-2026 Meta rule: business verification + privacy URL
  before templates).

## Acceptance criteria
- Embedded Signup exchange persists an encrypted per-tenant token (real/sandbox,
  fail-not-skip — DR-15).
- Status-gate: send is blocked when status != `live`.
- Callback derives tenant from the VT-289 nonce, not the URL.
- Zero-paste after the owner approves (CL-421).
- Dedicated number provisioned per tenant.

## Out of scope
- The inbound message pipeline + auto-reply (VT-287).
- The state-CSRF hardening itself (VT-289 — this row consumes it).
- Email/SMS hook channels (VT-288).
- Meta Tech Provider track approval — **Fazal initiates separately; do NOT block the
  build** (build against Embedded Signup / Twilio tech-provider API, canary vs
  sandbox/mock, live walk E2E-deferred like Shopify VT-283).
