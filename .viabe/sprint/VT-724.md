# VT-724 — owner email at onboarding

## Why this row exists
VT-715 shipped the DPDP consent-record email to prod. It works. But **nothing on either signup
surface collects an email**, so every consent record sits `record_email_sent_at IS NULL` —
"pending" — and the promise "every consent gets an emailed record" is currently unkept in
practice on WhatsApp signups (which is all of them).

**Fazal's ruling (2026-07-30):** ask at ONBOARDING, on BOTH surfaces — the web onboarding form
AND the WhatsApp onboarding journey. This overrides CC's recommendation of a post-activation
WhatsApp ask + optional portal field: the record should exist from the moment consent does, not
one activation later.

## Substrate that already exists (do NOT rebuild)
- `tenants.owner_email` — column present; `send_consent_record_email` already reads it and goes
  PENDING when null (`consent_record_email.py:84`).
- `send_pending_consent_record(tenant_id)` — the retroactive sender, `consent_record_email.py:130`.
  **It has ZERO callers today.** Wiring it at the capture point is part of this row's scope, not
  a follow-up.
- Both signup seams already fire the hook: `signup.py:240` (web) and `signup.py:331` (WhatsApp
  "I agree").

## Scope
1. **Web onboarding form** (`apps/team-web`) — an email field on the onboarding form. Validate
   syntax client- AND server-side. Persist to `tenants.owner_email`.
2. **WhatsApp onboarding journey** — one conversational ask, placed in the onboarding journey
   (not post-activation). Owner's language. Free-text reply → validate → confirm back → persist.
3. **Wire the retroactive send:** on any successful email capture, call
   `send_pending_consent_record(tenant_id)`. Fail-soft, idempotent (the existing
   `record_email_sent_at` stamp already guards re-send).
4. **Confirm-back before trusting it** (see the risk note): echo the captured address to the
   owner and accept a correction. Do NOT send a consent record to an address the owner has not
   seen echoed back.

## Risk note — Clau, flagged once (Fazal's call)
A mistyped email is worse than no email: the consent record — which names the business, carries
a masked phone and the DPDP/residency versions — lands in a stranger's inbox. That is a DPDP
disclosure incident, self-inflicted. Hence scope item 4 (echo-and-confirm) and syntax validation
before any send. Two further binding constraints:

- **Never gate onboarding on it.** Email is skippable on both surfaces; a refusal or a skip
  leaves the record pending exactly as today. Onboarding completion, activation and every
  correctness gate stay independent of email presence. WhatsApp onboarding is already long; a
  required field here buys drop-off in exchange for paperwork.
- **New PII, so new obligations:** `owner_email` must be covered by the DSR purge/anonymize path
  (verify — it lives on `tenants`, which DSR **anonymizes rather than deletes**, so confirm the
  anonymize routine actually clears it; the standing scar-tissue class), must not surface
  unencrypted to VTRs (PII-encrypted-from-VTR), and must not be echoed into logs.

## Exit gate
On deployed dev: (a) web onboarding captures + persists a valid email and the pending consent
record SENDS; (b) WhatsApp onboarding ask captures + echoes + persists, and the pending record
SENDS; (c) a malformed address is rejected on both surfaces with a retry, never persisted;
(d) skip/refusal path completes onboarding with the record still pending — no gate regression;
(e) DSR canary: purge a tenant, assert `owner_email` is gone/anonymized, not merely
FK-orphaned; (f) journey-touching → full-pack ×3.

## Boundaries
No new template unless the ask must go out-of-window — prefer the in-window free-form ask inside
the live onboarding journey. No real customer send (allowlist only). Prod promotion is Fazal's.
