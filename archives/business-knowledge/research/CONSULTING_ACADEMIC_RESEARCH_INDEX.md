# Consulting and academic business-research index

Created: 2026-07-26

Purpose: convert high-level business research into decision mechanisms that are usable by Viabe Team's Manager/COO and specialist-agent RAG. This is not a link dump. Each accepted source must contribute a situation, a decision rule, evidence to request, failure modes, an action, and a review or stop condition.

## Evidence policy

The publisher's prestige is not itself evidence. Use this order:

1. Field experiments, longitudinal datasets, administrative data, and peer-reviewed or clearly described working papers.
2. Consulting research that discloses the sample, comparison, method, or observed operating mechanism.
3. Primary operator doctrine and detailed cases, labelled as doctrine/case evidence rather than universal fact.
4. Practitioner synthesis with traceable primary references.
5. Discovery portals, search indexes, and commercial report listings only to find the original source.

Consulting findings are usually correlations or experience-based patterns, not universal causal laws. Cards therefore state the applicable context and the evidence the agent must collect before recommending action.

## Evaluation of the supplied discovery portals

| Portal | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| [HBS Faculty Publications](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/research/publications/Pages/default.aspx) and [Working Knowledge](https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/) | High-priority discovery source. | Strong route to working papers, cases, and readable research summaries. Card the original working paper or disclosed study, not a headline alone. |
| [Emerald Journal of Indian Business Research](https://www.emeraldgrouppublishing.com/journal/jibr) | High-priority India-context discovery source. | Useful journal scope and DOI metadata. Prefer open-access articles or lawful abstracts; record design, sample, country/sector, and limitations before extraction. |
| [Academia.edu Business](https://www.academia.edu/Documents/in/Business) | Discovery only. | Huge mixed-quality collection with weak topical precision and uncertain version-of-record status. Resolve title/DOI to the publisher, repository, or author manuscript first. |
| [SciSpace journal/year page](https://scispace.com/journals/business-research-28xg3pgw/2019) | Discovery only. | Aggregated ranking/summaries help find papers but are not the evidence layer. Verify journal identity, DOI, retractions, and original text. |
| [PaperGuide management list](https://paperguide.ai/papers/top/research-papers-business-management/) | Discovery only. | Curated/AI-mediated list with unclear selection quality. Use titles as leads, never as a source of business rules. |
| [HBR](https://hbr.org/) | Practitioner synthesis and case discovery. | High editorial value but often paywalled and not always empirical. Store short internal syntheses and links; do not bulk copy copyrighted articles or infer causality from anecdotes. |
| MarketResearch.com SMB/business-services category pages | Taxonomy and paid-report discovery only. | The category pages expose titles, vendors, dates, and snippets—not methods or underlying data. Do not train agents on CAGR or market-size claims from listings. Buy/license a report only for a defined decision and then record methodology and usage rights. |

## RAG routing decisions

- Manager/COO receives the decision lens: strategic fit, value at stake, evidence quality, decision rights, risk, capacity, trade-offs, ownership, review cadence, and stop conditions.
- Sales receives execution depth: channel design, coverage, qualification, pricing realization, peer coaching, account action, and handoff continuity.
- Marketing receives execution depth: journey orchestration, experimentation, feedback loops, message/channel consistency, attribution, and brand/customer learning.
- Finance receives value-baseline, benefits validation, resource-allocation, estimate, pricing, and cash-impact controls.
- Compliance is not made subordinate to consulting patterns. Legal/regulatory primary sources override all growth or transformation guidance.
- Source-specific numerical findings stay in provenance and limitations; the operational rule is written so an agent must collect local evidence before applying it.
