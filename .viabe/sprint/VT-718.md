# VT-718 — S2: the single emission choke

## Why
~7 surfaces speak to the owner today with partial context (signup machine, journey walker,
journey turn-brain, enforce gate, consent direct-handlers, paced-flow beats, OAuth resume
gates). Two-people drafting, the "No changes, looks good"→consent-decline misfire, boundary
double-replies and the taxonomy-vs-website contradiction are ONE disease: multiple mouths.

## Scope
EVERY owner-bound emission, from every surface, flows through ONE gate that has:
- wire-history-aware **dedup** (never re-say what the wire shows was just said),
- **contradiction block** (consults the S3 asserted-facts ledger when VT-719 lands; until then,
  wire-history contradiction detection),
- continuity/tone check (one voice, owner's language per VT-677).
**No bypass path, ever** — enforced by a CI gate in the style of
`gate-no-raw-railway-variables` (a new emission path that doesn't route through the choke
fails CI, not review).

## Boundaries
Deterministic correctness gates (consent/opt-out/approval/onboarded/money) are UNCHANGED and
still upstream — the choke governs the VOICE, never relaxes an effect gate. Brain-touching:
full-pack ×3 re-drive before landing.
