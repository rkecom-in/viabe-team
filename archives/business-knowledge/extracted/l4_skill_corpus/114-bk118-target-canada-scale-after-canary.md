## Situation

Target opened 133 Canadian stores rapidly amid inventory, data, system, training, price and assortment problems, then exited after deciding profitability was years away and estimating a large loss.

## Decision pressure

Leases, launch schedules and scale economics encourage opening the network before one representative flow works from item setup through shelf and purchase.

## Mistake or risk

Do not reduce a multi-cause failure to software or assume a foreign proposition transfers unchanged. The mechanism is scale before verified end-to-end readiness and local fit.

## Recommended next action

Run a real canary through item, supplier, order, receipt, replenishment, price, return and customer; set accuracy/availability thresholds; expand only after representative sites survive normal and exception cycles.

## Evidence needed

- Master-data accuracy and owner
- Forecast-to-shelf exceptions
- Shelf versus warehouse availability
- Local price and assortment research
- Readiness and unit economics
- Stage-gate evidence

## Red flags / escalation triggers

- Launch date cannot move
- Full warehouse but empty shelves
- Data cleanup follows scale
- Foreign-market fit is assumed

## Agent lesson

Specialists own data, customer and economics evidence; the Manager refuses network scale until a representative canary survives exceptions.

## Hard-gate candidate

Multi-site or multi-market rollout requires a real canary meeting data, availability, customer-fit and unit-economics thresholds.
