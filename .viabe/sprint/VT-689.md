## What

Every lane/agent module builds `_MODEL` at IMPORT (`resolve_chat_model` module-level — 10 sites:
orchestrator_agent, onboarding_conductor, integration_agent, sales/marketing/finance/accounting/
tech/cost_opt lanes). Violates the provider doctrine ("read FRESH every call — no import-time
freeze") and couples module IMPORT to provider credentials — surfaced at the VT-231 prod first
boot: OnboardingConductorModule's ctor imports the conductor for its tool list → ChatOpenAI ctor
→ missing OPENAI_API_KEY → startup exit.

## Judgment (why NOT fixed inline during promotion)

The failure mode is arguably CORRECT (config completeness enforced at boot, not first live turn),
Railway restarts on env change anyway (freeze is moot in practice), and touching 10 model-
construction sites is brain-adjacent → full re-gate. Debt, not emergency.

## Fix shape (when taken)

Lazy per-call resolve (module-level property/`_get_model()` accessor), preserving the current
default-argument seams (`build_*_node(model=...)`). One coherent PR; full-pack ×3 re-gate.
