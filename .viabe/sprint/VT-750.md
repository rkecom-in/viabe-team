# VT-750 — make the VT-723 tiers earned, or drop them to T4

## How this was established

Every one of the 33 cards was checked by opening its archived source snapshot and reading the
passage its `locator` points at. Not by re-reading the cards. The record is committed:

- `apps/team-orchestrator/knowledge_corpus/T4_CORROBORATION_VERIFICATION.md` — per-card verdicts + notes
- `apps/team-orchestrator/knowledge_corpus/t4_corroboration_verification.jsonl` — machine-readable
- `apps/team-orchestrator/knowledge_corpus/t4_corroboration_unsound_promotions.json` — the three

**6 of 33 are landing-grade.** `assert_corpus_verified` refuses the load while any card is not, and
it runs before the DB connection opens, so this row is what unblocks the corpus — not a preference.

## The three unsound promotions — fix these first, they are the row's product

A promotion out of `research_only` rests on independent qualifying clusters. For three claims the
clusters do not hold, which means the headline **15 candidate / 1 disputed / 2 absence is wrong**:

1. **`bk019` (bulky-product shipping, India-local-first)** — its two qualifying clusters are a USPS
   parcel tariff (inches, pounds, 108/130-inch length+girth, Zones 1-9 — none of it applicable in
   India) and a US SBA break-even page where `freight`, `postage`, `carrier`, `dimension` and
   `fulfil` each return **0 hits**. The break-even arithmetic is real and transfers; the shipping
   conclusion is corroborated by neither source.
2. **`bk031` (partner-network co-selling borrows trust)** — the two "independent" clusters are Cai &
   Szeidl field experiments in the same Chinese province, and **one paper cites the other**. That is
   one research program counted twice. Neither studies co-selling. Worse, the trust claim is
   attached to the paper that does NOT contain a trust result — the trust game is in the other one.
3. **`bk032` (brand memory, proof and promise)** — both clusters are opinion posts. One is a
   five-paragraph blog deferring to the book it sells; the other **is not the report its URL names**.
