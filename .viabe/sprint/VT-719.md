# VT-719 — S3: the asserted-facts ledger

## Why
The Manager must remember what it has SAID and COMMITTED, not just what it knows. A durable
record of assertions is the only way "never contradict yourself" is enforceable rather than
hoped for.

## Scope
- Durable, tenant-scoped store of facts/commitments the Manager has told the owner
  (assertion, when, in which turn, derived from which evidence/card version).
- **Consulted at compose time** (inside the VT-718 choke): a new statement that contradicts a
  prior assertion is blocked or must be framed as an owned change.
- **Owned changes:** "earlier I said X — that's now Y because…" is the ONLY legal way to flip a
  prior assertion. Silent flips are a defect, not a style issue.
- Privacy: tenant-scoped (L1/L2 posture), RLS, DSR `_PURGE_ORDER` registration in the SAME
  migration (the standing scar-tissue rule), PII-redacted.

## The O8 join (binding — one system, not two)
Per CL-2026-07-28-o8-living-knowledge §12.3: when an O8 card supersedes/expires/flips, the
system must identify the asserted facts DERIVED from the old card version, and surface the
correction. Design the `card_version ↔ asserted_fact` provenance join NOW, build it when this
row lands. Clau owns keeping the two specs aligned.
