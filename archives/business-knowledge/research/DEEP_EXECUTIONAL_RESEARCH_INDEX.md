# Deep executional business-research index

Created: 2026-07-26

This tranche prioritizes original research that can answer four questions: what was changed, in what setting, what happened, and where transfer can fail. Discovery portals such as Academia.edu, SciSpace, PaperGuide, HBR indexes, Emerald, and MarketResearch.com remain lead generators; they are not treated as evidence when the original method or text cannot be verified.

## Archived originals and RAG decisions

| RAG card | Archived original | Design and context | Executional knowledge retained | Transfer boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BK081 | `management/nber_does_management_matter_india_w16658.pdf` | Randomized management-consulting field experiment in large Indian textile firms. | Install linked measurement, review, ownership, quality, inventory, and delegation routines as an operating system; prove adoption in live work. | The reported 11% average productivity effect is not an SME or cross-sector promise. |
| BK082 | `management/nber_hybrid_work_w30292.pdf` | RCT with 1,612 graduate technology, marketing, and finance employees. | Set hybrid policy by role/workflow; track retention, output, communication, manager burden, inclusion, and time displacement. | One large technology firm; manager and non-manager responses differed materially. |
| BK083 | `management/nber_employee_recognition_india_w35138.pdf` | Firm-level RCT in India comparing worker vote, manager discretion, and random recognition. | Recognition allocation changes what workers value: attendance, productivity, discussion, social exchange, and culture can move differently. | Do not reduce the result to “democracy” or “manager choice” universally winning. |
| BK084 | `management/nber_manager_worker_matching_w27006.pdf` | Worker-manager administrative data and production analysis in six Indian garment factories. | Strong-manager rescue allocation can rationally protect powerful customer relationships while sacrificing system productivity; expose and reduce the dependency. | Counterfactual 1–4% productivity estimate is firm/context specific. |
| BK085 | `management/springer_information_overload_2019.pdf` | Peer-reviewed bibliometric and snowball literature review. | Design decision packets around the choice, relevance, contradictions, uncertainty, owner, and action; attach rather than lead with detail. | Heterogeneous underlying methods; card is a decision-design synthesis, not a causal field result. |
| BK086 | `sales/harvard_quota_frequency_sales.pdf` | Sales-compensation field experiment in a Swedish retail chain. | Frequent quotas can help low performers yet harm high performers and product mix; measure profit, returns, timing, and post-period effects. | Retail products and short-cycle selling; do not copy cadence to long-cycle B2B. |
| BK087 | `sales/harvard_incentives_reciprocity_sales.pdf` | Field experiment at an Asian consumer-durables seller. | Conditional bonuses and delayed reciprocal rewards are distinct contracts with heterogeneous and decaying effects; inspect future performance. | A reward called a gift may not create reciprocity; local culture and expectations matter. |
| BK088 | `management/hbs_jagged_ai_frontier.pdf` | Peer-reviewed preregistered experiment with 758 BCG knowledge workers. | Map AI at task level, test boundary cases, and preserve accountable verification; average productivity gains cannot authorize an entire workflow. | Tasks used one model generation and consulting context; the frontier moves with models and workflows. |
| BK089 | `marketing/nber_digital_advertising_w30925.pdf` | Yelp randomized field experiment across 18,294 restaurants; 7,209 received ads. | Paid advertising effectiveness is heterogeneous and can complement rating, reviews, and organic traffic; audit readiness and measure incrementality. | Platform, restaurant, geography, and purchase-intention outcomes constrain transfer. |
| BK090 | `marketing/nber_online_listings_w30810.pdf` | Temporal variation and a natural experiment adding more than 1,000 restaurants to a platform. | Verified online presence is minimum local-marketing infrastructure; join listing accuracy, response, reputation, and revenue measurement. | Estimated 5% revenue change is not a universal platform or sector result. |
| BK091 | `marketing/hbs_tradeoff_transparency.pdf` | Large randomized field experiment with 393,036 bank prospects. | Balanced trade-off disclosure can improve choice fit, usage, retention, and customer wellbeing without increasing acquisition. | Regulated financial products and experienced customers; promotion attenuated some effects. |
| BK092 | `marketing/hbs_operational_transparency.pdf` | Two field and two laboratory food-service experiments. | Carefully revealing real work can make effort credible and connect employees with beneficiaries; measure objective quality and employee effects. | Food-service visibility is not permission to expose private, unsafe, complex, or staged operations. |
| BK093 | `marketing/nber_promotional_reviews_w18340.pdf` | Empirical comparison of open and booking-verified hotel-review platforms. | Build verified customer proof, prohibit fabrication/competitor attacks, and investigate incentive-linked anomalies without declaring every anomaly fraudulent. | The study observes patterns consistent with manipulation, not the true author of each review. |

## Metadata-only leads deliberately not converted into claims

- HBS research on intrinsic motivation and internal referrals: the direct publisher download rejected automation. Retain as a lead until a lawful full-text version is verified; the abstract alone is insufficient for a detailed play.
- HBS work on internal versus external responsibility transparency and increasing search: publisher endpoints rejected automation. The reported abstracts are useful leads, not local evidence.
- Emerald India SME cases and articles: useful sector/DOI metadata, but most discovered full texts were licensed or paywalled. No paywall was bypassed and no listing snippet was converted into a causal card.
- MarketResearch.com category pages: useful taxonomy and paid-report discovery only. Market sizes, forecasts, and tactics require the licensed report and its methodology.
- Academia.edu and PaperGuide lists: broad and noisy discovery layers. Titles must resolve to a publisher, DOI, repository manuscript, or author copy before extraction.

## Specialist routing

- Manager/COO: BK081–BK085 and BK088 provide operating-system, workforce, incentives/culture, capacity, information-design, and AI-governance judgment. The Manager sees commercial cards only as risk/benefit decision lenses.
- Sales: BK084, BK086, BK087, BK089–BK092 provide talent allocation, compensation mechanics, paid-demand readiness, discoverability, customer fit, and proof tactics.
- Marketing: BK089–BK093 provide incremental acquisition, local discovery, trade-off messaging, operational proof, and authentic reputation systems.
- Finance: BK081, BK086, BK087, and BK089 require benefit validation, realized contribution, future-period effects, and incremental advertising economics.
- Compliance: BK091 and BK093 cover material offer disclosure and deceptive-review controls. Mutable law and platform policy must still be checked before deterministic action.
