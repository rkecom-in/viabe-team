## Situation

Domino's used harsh customer criticism while relaunching its recipe. Its 2010 annual report connects strong US same-store sales with the improved product, advertising and operational execution.

## Decision pressure

A bold confession or viral creative looks like the lever, so a business may amplify criticism before fixing the experience.

## Mistake or risk

Do not infer causality from the campaign alone or imitate self-criticism as theatre. The intervention bundled product, message and operations.

## Recommended next action

Turn recurring criticism into a tested product change; prepare delivery consistency and recovery; communicate the problem, verifiable change and retry invitation; measure repeat and complaint outcomes, not reach alone.

## Evidence needed

- Structured criticism by segment
- Before/after product test
- Delivery and recovery readiness
- Trial, repeat and complaint outcomes
- Incremental effect beyond discounts

## Red flags / escalation triggers

- Campaign precedes the fix
- Attention is the only success measure
- Operations cannot prove the promise
- Discount trial is confused with retention

## Agent lesson

Marketing owns evidence-backed repositioning, Sales closes the retry loop, and the Manager verifies that operations substantiate the promise.

## Hard-gate candidate

A reputation reset requires a verified product change, delivery readiness and repeat/complaint measurement.
