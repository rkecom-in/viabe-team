## The mechanism, file:line + causal chain

1. Owner asks for a campaign; triage starts an async `manager_task` for THIS turn
   (`skip_legacy_dispatch`) — that durable workflow OWNS the owner reply.
2. `runner.py` gives it a bounded head-start: `_D1_INTURN_WAIT_POLL_S = 4.0` ×
   `_D1_INTURN_WAIT_MAX_POLLS = 24` ≈ **96s** (`apps/team-orchestrator/src/orchestrator/runner.py`,
   the T9 inc-3 constants). The comment states the budget covers an *"observed spawn→SR→collapse→arm
   chain (~30-60s)"*.
3. **That premise is now false by ~4×.** The budget always exhausts.
4. D1 fires — `_COMPLETED_NO_REPLY_FALLBACK["en"] = "Got it — I'm on it and I'll update you
   shortly."` (`runner.py:161-164`, the VT-583 honest fallback).
5. The real draft + approval request lands ~5 minutes later, on a LATER turn — or never.
6. Anything asserting at that moment reads "no campaigns row" and calls it a routing failure.

Transcript, `sr_consequential_bulk_send_requires_approval`:

The owner pushed back impatiently at minute five. That is what a real owner does.

## Where the ~390s goes — decomposed from `pipeline_steps` on dev

`sr_consequential_bulk_send_requires_approval` (the 416s case), tenant `5f66dbc6`. Two webhook runs:

```
run A   21:41:34  webhook_received
        21:42:20  self_evaluate_gate                (+46s)
        21:42:25  campaign_plan_emitted             (+51s)

run B   21:46:55  webhook_received
        21:50:23  error: schema_rejection          (+208s)   <-- the bulk of the wall clock
        21:50:42  self_evaluate_gate
        21:51:04  self_evaluate_gate
        21:51:04  error: self_eval_rejected
```

**So it is not one slow model call — it is rejection-and-retry.** A schema rejection 208s in, then a
self-eval rejection, each costing a full round trip. Error census across the whole S1 window (3h):
**4 `schema_rejection` + 1 `self_eval_rejected`** out of 5 error steps, against 55
`webhook_received`. `schema_rejection` is the dominant error class on this path.

That reframes the fix: the first question is not "why is the chain slow" but **"why is the model
emitting output that fails schema validation on the SR draft path, and why does a rejection cost
~200s"**.

## The instrumentation cannot answer this yet, and that is a finding of its own

`pipeline_steps` HAS `started_at`, `ended_at`, `duration_ms`. Measured on dev:

- last 24h: **129 rows, 0 with `ended_at`, 0 with a non-zero `duration_ms`**
- all time: 959 rows, 100 populated — and **every one of those 100 is `step_kind = mcp_tool_call`**,
  the newest dated **2026-05-27**.

So this is **not a regression**: the duration fields were only ever written by the MCP tool-call
path. No step kind in the manager/SR chain has EVER recorded a duration. The substrate that would
answer "where does the time go" exists as columns and has no writer for the steps that matter — which
is why gate (a) below is "instrument", not "query". (I initially read this as instrumentation going
dark and checked before writing it down; the per-day and per-kind breakdown says otherwise.)

`llm_call_events` IS alive — 131 rows in the last 24h, newest during this window — but **none
attributed to this tenant** despite the chain plainly making model calls. Narrow enough to be a
separate check, not a claim: either attribution is missing on this path or the calls are recorded
without the tenant.

## Three separate defects, and they must not be conflated

**1. The chain is slow.** ~390s for spawn→SR→draft→arm. Unknown internal distribution — that is
step 1 of the scope, and it is NOT "raise the budget": a 400s in-turn wait would hang every turn.

**2. The promise has no handler.** D1 says *"I'll update you shortly."* In **3 of 6** measured asks
nothing followed, ever. A content-free ack that promises follow-up and then goes silent is worse than
an honest "I couldn't do that" — the owner has no reason to retry and no signal that it failed. This
is the `copy-promises-need-real-handlers` class.

**3. `assert_route` is unsound as written**, and it has been corrupting every gate that used it. It
concludes "did not delegate" from "no `campaigns` row right now". In pass 1 that was **FALSE for 3 of
the 4** scenarios it flagged. **The retracted 24% / 33% / 40% figures are very likely this artifact**,
not a routing defect — which means the numbers quoted in `GO-LIVE-BLOCKERS.md` B1 and the promotion
package are measuring latency and calling it delegation.

## Scope, in this order

0. **Chase the `schema_rejection` first — it is 208s of the 416s.** Why does the SR draft path emit
   schema-invalid output, and why does one rejection cost ~200s? That is the largest single term and
   it may make the rest moot.
1. **Instrument the chain before touching any constant.** Per-stage timings — triage → task spawn →
   SR delegation → cohort collapse → draft compose → approval arm → owner emission — on deployed
   dev, ×3. This means **writing `ended_at`/`duration_ms` for the step kinds that never had them**
   (only `mcp_tool_call` ever did), not querying what is already there. Publish the distribution.
   Only then decide what is reducible.
2. **Fix the broken promise, independent of the latency.** If D1 fires, the follow-up must be
   guaranteed to land or the owner must be told it failed. A promise emitted by
   `_COMPLETED_NO_REPLY_FALLBACK` needs a durable handler that either delivers or retracts.
3. **Make `assert_route` settle before it asserts** (poll to terminal, or state plainly that it is
   asserting LATENCY, not routing). Then re-run pass 1 and show what the routing-miss count actually
   becomes. Until this lands, no gate number about "delegation" can be trusted — including any I
   report.
4. **Re-quote the affected numbers** in `GO-LIVE-BLOCKERS.md` B1 + the promotion package once (1)-(3)
   are measured, per VT-738 gate (e).
5. **The product decision, if ~390s proves irreducible:** the ack must carry substance — cohort size,
   what it is doing, when to expect it — instead of "shortly". An owner waiting six minutes at a
   decisive moment is the O0 bar, not a harness artifact. Fazal's call on the copy; the mechanism is
   ours.

## What is NOT broken, and must stay that way

**The approval gate HELD in all three completed cases** — campaign `proposed`, task `waiting_owner`,
nothing sent. This row is about reliability and honesty, not safety. A fix that speeds the chain by
weakening the approval gate is the worst possible outcome (VT-738's own words).
