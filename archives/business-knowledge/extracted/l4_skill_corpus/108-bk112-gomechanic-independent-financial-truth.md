## Situation

During fundraising, GoMechanic investors said founders misstated facts including revenue and appointed an investigator; a founder acknowledged errors, followed by major layoffs and a distressed acquisition.

## Decision pressure

Valuation and fundraising deadlines can make unaudited management reporting feel like an acceptable bridge.

## Mistake or risk

Do not diagnose fraud from a variance or use reporting for legal conclusions. The transferable failure is weak separation between growth ownership, financial truth and independent assurance.

## Recommended next action

Define recognition and cut-off; reconcile contracts, invoices, delivery, collections, bank and tax records; give Finance independent authority; investigate unexplained gaps and correct numbers before financing decisions.

## Evidence needed

- Customer-level ledger and policy
- Contracts, delivery and collections
- Bank, GST and statutory reconciliation
- Related parties and manual journals
- Independent investigation and remediation

## Red flags / escalation triggers

- Fundraising numbers bypass Finance
- Revenue grows without collections or tax evidence
- Management and statutory reports do not reconcile
- Pressure to recognise incomplete work

## Agent lesson

Finance and Compliance own verification; the Manager protects their independence from teams rewarded for growth.

## Hard-gate candidate

Financing and acquisition decisions require reconciled revenue, cash and tax evidence plus independent sign-off on material adjustments.
