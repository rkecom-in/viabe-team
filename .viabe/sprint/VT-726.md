# VT-726 — fill the registry enough to prove the pipe

## Why this row exists (CC's bounce, 2026-08-03 — correcting a Clau error)
I wrote VT-725 on the premise that 118 cards were retrieval-eligible. **They are not. Every O8
table has ZERO rows.** The 118 are FILES in the local-only `archives/business-knowledge/` corpus
that has never been ingested. CC verified against deployed dev and stopped rather than wire a
call site that would forever log "0 candidates". Independently confirmed by Clau against
`migrations/182`.

Three blockers, in biting order:
1. **Nothing to retrieve** — ingestion never ran.
2. **No embedding substrate for cards** — `CardRetrievalEngine.retrieve()` requires a
   `card_embeddings` mapping; vectors exist for `l1_entities` and `l4_documents` only.
   *Solvable in-process for shadow:* one batched embed over the eligible set, cached, fail-soft.
   No migration needed until `active`.
3. **The schema cannot round-trip a `KnowledgeCard`** — the decisive one. `knowledge_cards` is
   missing `domain`, and the engine's FIRST filter is `if domain not in profile.domains: raise`.
   `source_class` / `usage_rights` / `independence_cluster_id` are reconstructible by a 3-way
   join; **`domain` exists on no table at all.**

## Fazal's ruling
Option **B now, A mandatory after** — "seed a small corpus, prove the pipe, and once proved
ensure all 118 files are ingested. **The full ingestion must not be missed.**" The full
ingestion is rostered as **VT-727** so it cannot quietly evaporate once the pipe looks green.

## Scope

### 1. `domain` becomes a FIRST-CLASS COLUMN — migration 189 (allocated)
Not a 3-way join, not derived. The engine reads it on **every** retrieval as its first filter;
a join-per-filter is slower and fragile, and a derived value cannot be indexed for the
`(domain, status)` access pattern retrieval actually uses.
- `knowledge_cards.domain` NOT NULL, constrained to the `KnowledgeDomain` enum values.
- Index `(domain, status)`.
- GLOBAL table — **no tenant_id, no tenant data**. The §0 ownership rule is unchanged.
- Decide in the same migration whether `source_class` / `usage_rights` / `independence_cluster`
  / `retrieval_eligible` / `corpus_version_id` are denormalized onto the card or exposed via a
  read view. **Either is acceptable; a per-retrieval 3-way join is not.** State the choice and
  the reason in the migration header.
- Migration is WRITTEN by Codex, **EXECUTED by CC** under Fazal's authorization. Dev only.

### 2. Seed 10–20 cards — THROUGH THE PIPELINE, NOT BY INSERT
**Binding, and the thing most likely to be gotten wrong:** the seed cards MUST enter via the
VT-710 ingestion path — extraction, rights/originality gate, claim_key normalization,
independence clustering, applicability metadata, admission as `candidate` → `validated`.

**A hand-authored direct INSERT is forbidden.** That is precisely the authored-playbook
mechanism Track-D retired and Codex's R1 correction killed (`l4_corpus.py` stays retired). If we
seed by INSERT we will have rebuilt the retired thing wearing the new schema, and we will not
have proven the pipe — which is the entire point of this row.

Draw the seed from the existing corpus files (a subset of the 118), so the run is a genuine
rehearsal of VT-727 at small scale.

### 3. Card embeddings for shadow
In-process batched embed over the eligible set, cached, fail-soft. A card that fails to embed is
excluded from retrieval, never a failed turn. Persisted embedding storage is VT-727/active scope.

## Exit gate
- `knowledge_cards` holds 10–20 `validated` rows with `domain` populated, **every one of which
  came through the pipeline** — provable from lifecycle events, not asserted.
- A retrieval against a real tenant profile returns **non-zero** ranked candidates with a trace.
- The rights/originality gate demonstrably REJECTED at least one candidate, or an explicit note
  saying nothing in the seed subset warranted rejection and why.
- Global-purity check re-run: no tenant identifier in any global text field.
- Migration 189 applied to dev only; `--expected-env dev` guard honoured.

## Boundaries
Dev only. No prod migration. Retrieval stays **advisory** and authorizes nothing
(ARCHITECTURE §0.1.1). VT-725 (the consumer wiring) sits ON TOP of this row and unblocks the
moment the registry holds rows and `domain` is readable.
