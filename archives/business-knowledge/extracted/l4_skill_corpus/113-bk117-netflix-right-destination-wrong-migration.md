## Situation

Netflix's move toward streaming was defensible, but its 2011 price and Qwikster separation created friction and became a symbol of not listening. It reversed Qwikster after negative domestic net additions.

## Decision pressure

Leaders convinced about the destination may treat brand, account continuity, pricing sequence and workflow as execution details.

## Mistake or risk

Customer backlash does not prove the strategic destination was wrong, and not all subscriber movement can be attributed to Qwikster. Separate strategy from migration design.

## Recommended next action

Map customer discontinuities; test price, brand, account and billing changes separately; preserve continuity; stage cohorts; monitor cancellation reasons and support load; enable component-level rollback.

## Evidence needed

- Strategic case independent of migration
- Before/after customer journey
- Cohort cancellations and reasons
- Support and billing load
- Rollback boundaries and retained learning

## Red flags / escalation triggers

- Multiple painful changes launch together
- Internal structure dictates experience
- Rollback equals abandoning strategy
- Objections are dismissed as resistance

## Agent lesson

Sales and Marketing own customer continuity; the Manager keeps strategic direction and migration execution as separate decisions.

## Hard-gate candidate

Material migrations require staged cohorts, continuity, cancellation evidence and component-level rollback.

## Retrieval triggers

- Qwikster failure
- right strategy bad execution
- migration backlash
- price change churn
- separate brand migration
