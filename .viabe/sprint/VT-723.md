# VT-723 — grow corpus coverage from high-authority sources

## Re-scope note (read first)
This row previously said "replace the 96 unknown-rights cards". **That is superseded.** Fazal
ruled that inclusion turns on the knowledge's accuracy and value, not the source's licence —
copyright protects expression, not facts, and the cards are original expression of extracted
findings. The 96 are eligible. This row is now **additive**: strengthen the corpus where the
evidence is weak, not where the paperwork is missing.

## Scope
Distil NEW cards from high-authority sources — **World Bank** (Enterprise Surveys, MSME
reports, PRWPs + the microdata request in flight) · **NBER** · **J-PAL / IPA** / McKenzie
microenterprise work · **government/regulator** (GST, RBI, SIDBI) · **BIT / ideas42** — through
the VT-710 pipeline as candidates.

**Target the gaps O11 exposes**, not volume — seed where the baseline shows weak decision
slices. No card-count target. High-authority sources earn a stronger T-tier, which improves
retrieval ranking and conflict resolution; that is the reason to prefer them, not licensing.

## The check that replaced the rights gate
**Expression originality**: no card may contain verbatim or near-verbatim source text — it must
state the claim in our own words. Reject or rewrite otherwise. Also flag (not block) if one
source would dominate the corpus (compilation/database-rights concentration) or if a source's
ToS contractually prohibits extraction — those are the narrow real constraints.

## Unchanged
Raw archived pages stay local-only and never retrieval-eligible (they ARE reproductions) · the
5 `live_link_only` cards keep their honesty convention · admission still governed by §6 ·
provenance/source-class retained for AUTHORITY WEIGHTING.

## Sequencing
After the O11 baseline (it tells us where to seed). Not launch-blocking.
