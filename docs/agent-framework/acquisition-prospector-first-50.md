## Aggregate CTWA targeting brief for the Ad Composer

**Objective:** attract founder-led Indian F&B concepts that are 2–16 weeks from launch to a Viabe
Market Intelligence Feasibility report landing page.  
**Geography:** start with Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Hyderabad, Pune and Mumbai; test
Tier-2 clusters separately rather than blending CPM and intent.  
**Audience hypothesis:** ages 24–45; interests/context around food entrepreneurship, cloud kitchens,
restaurant management, D2C/FMCG, FSSAI, franchise ownership, commercial kitchens, packaging and
Shopify. Exclude existing customers and recent purchasers. Do not upload this prospect list as a
custom audience.  
**Creative promise:** “Before you sign the lease or print 10,000 packs, test demand, pricing,
competition and break-even assumptions for your exact city and format.”  
**Destination:** a tracked Reports Feasibility landing link minted by CC’s read-signal scheme; the
Ad Composer supplies UTM fields but cannot mint or publish it.  
**Success metric:** attributed completed Reports purchases, with tracked-link clicks as an
intermediate diagnostic only.  
**Kill criterion proposal:** stop an ad cell after the owner-approved spend threshold is reached
with zero attributed qualified landing visits, or after the larger owner-approved threshold with
zero purchases. The actual ₹ thresholds must come from the owner; the Prospector must not invent
them.
