## CLOSED — what the bulk-send "defect" actually was (2026-08-05 late, deployed dev)

Three separate things wearing one label. In order of discovery:

**(a) The reasoning-cap class (real defect, fixed above).** Pre-floor, manager review's extraction
(cap 600) came back empty on luna → fail-closed **`blocked`** → and `blocked` is deliberately
NON-terminal (operator-resolvable, `workflow.py` L59), so it permanently occupies the tenant's ONE
active-task slot. Every later objective is then admitted **`queued`** and `promote_next_queued_task`
never promotes it (promotion requires no active task). That is the wedge: `route='none'`, no campaign,
forever. Confirmed on the leftover tenants — step 1's task `blocked` at 22:07, step 2's admitted
`queued` at 22:09, same tenant.

**(b) My own concurrency during diagnosis.** Three x3 runs raced each other and exhausted the Seoul
pooler's 15-connection cap (`EMAXCONNSESSION`), which is itself a way to make a task block. The
standing rule (SERIAL shards only on Seoul) applies to diagnosis runs too, not just the pack.

**(c) The harness deadline sits UNDER the product's own maximum in-turn wait — the measurement bug.**
`run_critical_x3`'s step deadline defaulted to **90s**, while `runner._D1_INTURN_WAIT_MAX_POLLS ×
_D1_INTURN_WAIT_POLL_S` is **~96s**: when a turn starts an async manager_task the sync run
deliberately waits that long for the real reply to land in-turn (T9 inc-3 raised it from 15s so a fast
task answers in ONE beat). So any turn whose task does not answer quickly is recorded TIMEOUT **by
construction**. Raised the harness default to 180s (96s wait + turn work + headroom) in
`run_critical_x3.py` and `convo_harness.py`, with the reason written next to the constant.

**The proof that the product path is sound** — the same step 1, driven in isolation at a longer
window, on the fixed build:
- `→ owner I've drafted a campaign for 19 customers (lapsed-45d-plus) … reply to approve, and nothing
  goes out until you do.` + `Approve sending a recovery campaign to 19 customers?`
- `[internal route: sales_recovery]` — the delegation the scenario asserts
- DB: `manager_tasks.status = waiting_owner`, `campaigns.status = proposed`, **`campaign_messages = 0`**
- ONE beat, no 96s silence, no timeout.

So the bulk always-confirm floor is **verified**, not unverified: the draft/approval gate holds and
zero customer messages were sent. My predecessor's retracted "90s harness deadline under the product's
96s wait" theory was RIGHT for this scenario and wrong only in its generalisation — it was retracted
because measured *successful* turns maxed at 62.9s, but those are turns whose reply lands in-turn and
therefore never spend the full budget.

**Still a product question (not fixed here):** on the first turn of a genuinely slow job the owner
gets nothing for ≥96s. The candidate fix stays what I described — make the budget a DEADLINE FROM TURN
START rather than 96s stacked on top of the turn's own work — which bounds owner silence without
costing the single-beat win. Not doing it blind: it trades against the double-beat the judge reads as
loop_stall, and it should be measured, not assumed.

**Also worth Fazal's eye:** one `blocked` objective wedges the tenant permanently — the owner can
never start another. That is deliberate (operator-resolvable), but on a live tenant it is silent and
unbounded.

## Correction to the handoff's characterisation of this defect

The re-drive log (`canaries/reports/vt720_sr_redrive.log`, all 3 runs) shows: **step 2 PASSES every
time** with the bulk always-confirm floor visibly working (draft + "Approve sending a recovery
campaign to 19 customers?" + "nothing goes out until you do"). So the floor is NOT unverified — it is
observed working 3×; what is unverified is step 1's own side-effect assertions. And step 1 is a
TIMEOUT, not a silent drop: its reply lands out-of-band during step 2, which the late-reply sweep
catches. Mechanism: on the first turn the sync run waits the full ≈96s D1 in-turn budget before
emitting the honest ack, and turn-work + 96s exceeds the harness's 150s window; on step 2 a task is
already active so the ack is immediate. The product defect inside that is bounded-silence (≥96s with
nothing), and the candidate fix is to make the budget a DEADLINE FROM TURN START rather than 96s
stacked on top of the turn's own work — NOT a blind re-tune of the constant T9 inc-3 deliberately
raised.
