## Situation

The Federal Reserve found SVB grew rapidly with concentrated, largely uninsured deposits and inadequate interest-rate and liquidity management; a connected depositor base then withdrew at extraordinary speed.

## Decision pressure

Growth and stable recent behaviour make concentrated funding and long-duration assets look cheaper than resilience.

## Mistake or risk

Do not transplant bank ratios into an SMB. Transfer the concentration, maturity and correlated-behaviour logic.

## Recommended next action

Map customer, supplier, lender, bank and funding concentrations; identify parties likely to act together; time inflows against fixed outflows; stress sudden loss; pre-arrange diversified liquidity and triggers.

## Evidence needed

- Revenue and funding concentration
- Contract and cash timing
- Correlated-party behaviour
- Immediate cash under stress
- Backup liquidity and activation owner

## Red flags / escalation triggers

- One ecosystem controls most inflow
- Callable cash funds long commitments
- Assumptions are weakened to pass
- Growth outpaces risk capacity
