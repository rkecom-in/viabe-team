## Read this before submitting — three things that will get a template rejected or misused

1. **These are MARKETING, not UTILITY.** Both templates are business-initiated promotion to someone
   who is not in an open session. Submitting them as UTILITY to dodge the marketing limits would be
   a miscategorisation Meta polices, and it would put our approved UTILITY templates at risk. Submit
   as **MARKETING** and accept the per-user marketing cap.
2. **Approval is NOT permission to send.** A send still requires a recorded consent basis per lead
   (FIRST-PROOF red line 2 and P1.2: no basis ⇒ row excluded and counted). **P1.3 — your written
   answer on what the Reports funnel actually captured — governs how many of our leads are
   contactable at all.** An approved template with no consent basis is still a template we may not
   send.
3. **The URL button differs per template and one of them has a dependency.** `lead_winback1` uses the
   **customer-scoped** `/r/<token>` link (VT-745 infra — exists today). `campaign_offer1` needs an
   **aggregate, campaign-scoped** destination, which does not exist yet — that is **VT-773**. The
   copy below is submittable now either way; only `campaign_offer1`'s live send waits on VT-773.

## Template 1 — `lead_winback1`

**Category:** MARKETING · **Languages:** `en`, `hi` · **Button:** URL (dynamic suffix)
**Variable signature (positional):** `1 = lead_name` · `2 = business_name` · `3 = enquired_about`
**Button URL:** `https://viabe.ai/r/{{1}}` — dynamic suffix, `1 = tracked_token`

### en

> Hi {{1}}, this is {{2}}. You asked us about {{3}} a while back and we never picked the thread up
> again — no problem at all. If it's still useful to you, everything you need is on one page.
> Tap below to take a look.

**Footer:** Reply STOP to unsubscribe.
**Button label:** View the details

### hi (Devanagari)

> नमस्ते {{1}}, मैं {{2}} से हूँ। आपने कुछ समय पहले {{3}} के बारे में पूछा था और फिर बात आगे नहीं
> बढ़ पाई — कोई बात नहीं। अगर यह अब भी आपके काम का है, तो पूरी जानकारी एक ही पेज पर है।
> देखने के लिए नीचे टैप करें।

**Footer:** बंद करने के लिए STOP भेजें।
**Button label:** जानकारी देखें

**Why the copy is shaped this way.** It names the sender in the first line (Meta expects the business
to be identifiable, and a lead who enquired months ago will not recognise an unnamed number). It
states the lapse plainly and absolves the lead — "no problem at all" — because the alternative
registers as a reprimand for not buying. It makes **no claim about the product**: no discount, no
urgency, no "limited time", nothing that would need a fact to stand behind it. That is deliberate and
it is the judgment-vs-citation law applied to outbound copy — the page can make the case, the
template must not invent one.

## Template 2 — `campaign_offer1`

**Category:** MARKETING · **Languages:** `en`, `hi` · **Button:** URL (dynamic suffix)
**Variable signature (positional):** `1 = customer_name` · `2 = business_name` ·
`3 = offer_description` · `4 = valid_until`
**Button URL:** `https://viabe.ai/c/{{1}}` — dynamic suffix, `1 = campaign_token` (**VT-773**)

### en

> Hi {{1}}, {{2}} here. We're running something you might want to know about: {{3}}. It's on until
> {{4}}. Tap below for the details.

**Footer:** Reply STOP to unsubscribe.
**Button label:** See the offer

### hi (Devanagari)

> नमस्ते {{1}}, {{2}} की ओर से। आपके लिए एक जानकारी है: {{3}}। यह {{4}} तक उपलब्ध है।
> विवरण के लिए नीचे टैप करें।

**Footer:** बंद करने के लिए STOP भेजें।
**Button label:** ऑफ़र देखें

**Why `{{3}}` and `{{4}}` are separate variables.** The offer text and its end date are the two things
that vary per campaign, and keeping the date as its own variable stops the offer string from
absorbing a claim about time that nothing verifies. `{{4}}` must be rendered from the campaign's own
stored end date, never composed by a model.
