# Fazal baseline — 25 India-SMB judgment scenarios (BLIND instrument)

**Authored by Clau, 2026-08-11. Contains NO answers, by design.**

## What this is for

We hold **zero** verified human business judgment (CL-2026-08-11). The 825 calibration entries were
answered by a third-party AI role-playing a Business Head, and the scenarios were US-enterprise, so
licensing them buys a validated corpus we still cannot use.

These 25 replace them. They answer a **strategic** question, not a data-hygiene one:

> **Does a generic frontier model's business judgment match Fazal's, in OUR domain?**
>
> **High agreement** → our edge is NOT judgment. Stop corpus work; put everything into execution and
> tenant outcome data. Saves months.
> **Low agreement** → Fazal's judgment is distinctive, is a real Tier-1 seed, and systematic capture
> is justified.

Either result changes a decision. That is the whole reason to spend Fazal's time here.

## Protocol — deviate and the measurement is void

1. **Fazal answers first, cold.** No AI answers exist yet. Nothing to anchor on.
2. **Then Codex answers the same 25, blind** — prompted only "you are the business head of an Indian
   SMB, choose and justify." Codex is arms-length and unaware of purpose, mirroring exactly how the
   original 825 were produced, so it is a fair test of the same question. **Codex must never see
   Fazal's answers.**
3. **Compare on the DECISION RULE, not the option number.** Two people can pick the same option for
   different reasons or different options for the same reason. The rationale is the data; the option
   number is a label. Scoring: `same rule` / `compatible rule, different tactic` / `different rule`.
4. **Honest statistical limit, stated up front: N=25 can REJECT decisively, it cannot CERTIFY.** At
   20/25 the 95% interval runs roughly 59–93%. Enough to kill an idea, not enough to bless one.

## Design notes (so a future reader can audit the instrument)

- **Stratified:** pricing/margin · cash/working-capital · customer/retention · marketing/acquisition ·
  operations/supply · people/partners · risk/ethics.
- **Every scenario has genuine tension.** A scenario with an obvious answer produces agreement that
  measures nothing. If Fazal finds any of these obvious, that itself is worth flagging.
- **Option order is deliberately shuffled** — the "sophisticated" choice is not consistently placed.
- Hybrids are allowed and encouraged: answer `Option 3 + 5` if that is the real call.
- All figures are ₹, at ₹2–20 Lakh/month revenue scale.

## How to answer

For each: the option number(s), then **one or two lines of WHY**. The why is the part that matters —
without it we are comparing labels. "None of these, I'd do X" is a valid and valuable answer.
