## Two findings that matter MORE than the counts

### (1) Independence clustering has never actually collapsed anything — and it is load-bearing
118 cards across 104 clusters; 7 clusters hold multiple cards. **Zero cards were collapsed.** All
118 claim keys are unique, and dedup requires cluster + claim_key + claim_value to match, so exact
dedup removes nothing.

More seriously: **clustering currently follows SOURCE IDENTITY, not semantics.** It has never been
shown to detect the same study retold through different URLs — which is the entire point of the
mechanism. Corroboration count is therefore **not yet evidence of independent confirmation**, and
corroboration feeds ranking, conflict resolution and the T4 promotion rule ("two independent
clusters"). A T4 card could be promoted by two retellings of one thread.

**A cross-source semantic/manual cluster audit is IN SCOPE for this row and is a gate, not a
nice-to-have.** Until it passes, no corroboration-dependent promotion is trustworthy.

### (2) Authority inflation: Reddit threads were tiered T1v (vendor-policy)
Of 5 T1v cards, **2 are genuine platform policy (WhatsApp, Google Business Profile) and 3 are
Reddit threads.** Codex framed this as overbroad deferral. The sharper reading: **the classifier
put community discussion into the vendor-authoritative tier.** T1v means "authoritative about that
platform's rules." A Reddit thread is T4 experiential.

Tier drives ranking AND conflict resolution (§7: vendor-policy outranks practitioner and
experiential within its domain). A mis-tiered card does not merely rank too high — **it can win an
adjudication against a correct lower-tier card.** Fix the classification; do not fix the deferral.
