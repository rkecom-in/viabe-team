# VT-774 — the tenant tells us nothing; the product finds out and asks

## The rule this row exists to obey

**RKEcom is a pure tenant** (CL-2026-08-20). No founder side-channel: no answers in chat, no
Fazal-assembled export, no insider credentials in an agent data path. The owner's role reduces to
exactly two in-product actions — **grants** and **attestations**. Everything else the agents need,
they discover.

Fazal's own test for whether we built it right: *"If we are able to build a fully dynamic Viabe Team
for Rkecom, we will be able to offer it to any other business."* So the measure of this row is not
that RKEcom's leads got imported — it is that they got imported **through a path a stranger's
business could have walked**.

## Scope A — discovery → propose → grant → ingest (the P1.2 connector)

1. **Discover** where the tenant's customers/leads live. Public surfaces + whatever the tenant has
   already connected; the agent proposes what it found rather than presenting a blank questionnaire
   (CL-421's spirit: zero manual paste, and a form the owner must fill is the same customer-hostile
   shape one layer up).
2. **Propose** the connection on the tenant channel, in the tenant's language, with what it will read
   and why.
3. **Grant** — OAuth/credential tap. **This is the ONE owner action** and it is irreducible: consent
   to access cannot be discovered, only given.
4. **Ingest** through a **generic lead-source connector**. Every row carries **consent basis + source
   + date**; a row with no basis is **excluded and counted**, never imported quietly.

**The forbidden shortcut, named so it cannot be rationalised later:** reading the Reports lead store
directly with our own credentials because we happen to own it. That would import the cohort and prove
nothing — the connector would be untested exactly where a real tenant depends on it. **If the
connector cannot reach it, a real tenant could not have either**, and that is the finding, not an
obstacle to route around.

## Scope B — the consent attestation (the P1.3 flow), which is a STEP of Scope A, not a sibling

1. **Discovery drafts it.** The agent reads the funnel surfaces — enquiry form copy, the phone
   field's own label, any checkbox or notice, the privacy-policy paragraph covering contact, and form
   version history across the capture window — plus the lead store's per-row source/version.
2. **Cohort-by-cohort proposal, judgment-vs-citation.** Per cohort: what the lead actually SAW,
   **quoted**, with **where it lives** cited; what channel and purpose that text supports; and a
   **per-template ruling**. Those rulings differ and must not be blurred: a bare enquiry plausibly
   supports a **follow-up about the thing enquired about** (`lead_winback`), and usually does **NOT**
   support **unrelated promotional offers** (`campaign_offer`). Cohorts with no discoverable basis are
   **excluded and counted**.
3. **The Manager asks on the tenant's WhatsApp channel** and captures confirm/correct per cohort.
   Complex detail may link out (in-app page, return to chat — CL-443), but **the ask and the answer
   live on the tenant channel.** No doc, no chat side-channel.
4. **The captured answer is the recorded basis** — a tenant attestation, per-cohort, timestamped,
   with the discovery evidence attached so it is auditable rather than merely asserted.

**Why the tenant must answer even though we drafted it:** under DPDP the tenant is the fiduciary. Our
discovery is *evidence*; only the tenant can *attest*. The CEO seat cannot waive this for RKEcom —
the gates bind us hardest, which is the entire point of tenant zero.

## Scope containment (pre-agreed, so this does not become a platform project)
**Dynamic in SHAPE, bounded in BUILD.** The discovery/propose/grant/attest flow is generic and is
built once. The proof window ships **ONE real connector — Reports — through that generic surface.**
Do not build a universal integration engine before September.

## Exit gate
(a) RKEcom's leads are imported with **no Fazal-side export and no insider credential anywhere in the
path** — proven by the connector being the only reader. (b) Every imported row carries basis + source
+ date; basis-less rows are excluded **and counted in the report**, not dropped silently. (c) The
consent ask and its answer are both on the tenant WhatsApp channel, and the attestation persists
per-cohort with evidence attached. (d) `campaign_offer` is NOT authorised by a cohort whose only basis
is a bare enquiry — the per-template ruling is enforced at send, not left to the drafter. (e) A second
lead source could be added by configuration rather than by new code — the generic-shape test.
