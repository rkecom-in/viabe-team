  6. 2

    For context: i'm talking about an e-mail marketing agency (righthello.com, deceased) and a B2B consulting company (Casbeg.com, alive and well).

    Both times we did it the same way. We've built a simple website to enforce understanding what are we offering (you think you know what you are offering, until you need to write it down in a sales offer). Once we had that we've posted it on our private social media (even then we had a little bit of following but nothing huge) and sent out the "Hello World" post to couple of relevant contacts with "hey, i'm starting a new business, based on what you do this might be of interest to You. <link to the post>".

    And we were off to the races. Both times it got us first ~10 paying customers.

    Hey Frank

    This applies to ALL of my businesses and experiences. Doesn't matter if B2C or B2B.

    The VERY first customers are all earned through direct conversations. Cold calls. Friends of friends. Direct meetings. Direct calls. Manual work.

    My first project was a CPA network that I launched in 2013. At that time, there were many active niche forums where people gathered, primarily using forums rather than social media. I created threads on all available forums and managed to acquire a few initial clients.
