# VT-744 — capture is cheap, and it is irreversible if missed

## Why this is Critical when nothing reads it

Judgment is commodity (88%, two models). A knowledge corpus adds nothing (O11 null). **The only
knowledge that is genuinely ours is what actually happened to real tenants** — and we are about to
run our first real ones without recording it properly.

**Capture is cheap. Not capturing is permanent.** The learning loop can be built next year against
this year's data. A year we did not record cannot be re-observed. Of everything in `MOAT.md` this is
the item most likely to be skipped and most expensive to skip — precisely because **nothing consumes
it in V1**, which makes it feel optional. It is not.

## STEP 1 — AUDIT FIRST. Do not build until this is done.

**This week produced four wrong blockers because status came from documents instead of code.** So
this row starts with an inventory, not a schema.

Substrate that likely already captures part of this — **verify each, do not assume**:
`tm_audit` · `pipeline_runs` / `pipeline_steps` · `owner_message_audit` · `owner_notifications`
(mig 150) · `agent_drafts` · `agent_corrections` (mig 154) + `agent_corrections_pairs` (mig 160) ·
`campaign_messages` / `campaign_recipients` · `manager_tasks` (mig 151) · `decision_evidence_links` ·
`o8_tenant_evidence` (mig 183) · `llm_cost_audit` (mig 173) · `conversation_log.py` ·
`send_idempotency_keys` · `debug_events`.

**Deliverable of step 1: a gap table** — for each field in §Step 2, state `CAPTURED (where)` /
`PARTIAL (what is missing)` / `NOT CAPTURED`. **Close only the gaps.** If most of it already exists,
this row is small and that is the best outcome.

## STEP 2 — the six things that must be recorded per tenant

1. **The ACTION** — what was done, when, and **on whose authority**: autonomous · owner-approved ·
   VTR-directed. Authority is the field most often lost and the one that makes everything else
   interpretable later.
2. **The CONTEXT at decision time** — what the Manager knew when it decided: tenant state, active
   objective, plan step, capability invoked. Reconstructing this after the fact is impossible; the
   inputs must be stamped at the moment of the decision.
3. **The OUTCOME** — delivered · read · replied · clicked · opted out · blocked · failed, and the
   **business result where knowable** (order placed, amount, none). VT-741's read/click/reply
   signals are the substrate; this row consumes them, it does not rebuild them.
4. **The OWNER'S REACTION** — approved · **edited** · rejected · ignored. **An edit is the highest-value
   row in the entire ledger**: a human corrected a specific output for a specific reason. `agent_corrections`
   may already hold this — check before building.
5. **THE VTR INTERVENTION and its REASON.** A VTR overriding the Manager is a human disagreeing with
   a machine on a real case. **The reason is the lesson.** Free text is acceptable; a missing reason
   is not — an override with no reason is a lost lesson.
6. **What was NOT done** — the rejected alternative, where one was considered. Without it, later
   analysis has outcomes but no counterfactual and can only learn "what we did" not "what was better."

## Binding constraints

- **APPEND-ONLY and immutable.** An outcome is never rewritten. (CL-2026-08-04: a report bundle that
  merged two runs silently doubled 13 scenarios and would have double-scored the judge.) Corrections
  are new rows referencing the old, never edits.
- **Never on the critical path of a turn.** Capture failure degrades to a logged miss; it must NEVER
  fail a customer send, an owner reply, or a gate. Fail-open on capture, fail-closed on effects — the
  exact inverse of the send rules, and deliberately so.
- **Tenant-scoped, RLS + FORCE.**
- **Register in `_PURGE_ORDER` IN THE SAME MIGRATION.** DSR anonymizes the `tenants` row rather than
  deleting it, so no FK cascade will ever clean this up. This is the scar that recurs on every new
  PII table (episodic_events, L2, and nearly on VT-741's click table).
- **Store the tenant-scoped raw row.** Category normalisation (`Kirana_Tier2_City`) is for later
  cross-tenant use and is a DERIVED view — never a substitute for the raw row, and never a reason to
  drop tenant scoping at write time.
- **NO CONSUMER IN V1.** No retrieval, no analysis, no learning, no dashboard. If a consumer appears
  in this row's PR, the scope has drifted. It is a recording, not a feature.

## Explicitly OUT of scope
The §12 learning half — mutation of cards from outcomes, cross-tenant aggregation, k-anonymity
rollups, DPO pair generation, any model update. All of it waits. **Building learning before there is
data to learn from is the exact "seed first, measure after" inversion that produced the O11 null.**

## Exit gate
(a) The **gap table** exists and is accurate against code — every claim cites a table or a file:line.
(b) A real owner turn on dev produces rows covering all six fields, or a stated, justified gap.
(c) An owner EDIT is captured with the before/after and is distinguishable from an approval.
(d) A VTR override is captured with its reason; an override with no reason is rejected at write time.
(e) Forced capture failure does NOT fail the turn — proven by forcing it, not by reading the `except`.
(f) DSR canary: purge a tenant, assert these rows are gone, not merely FK-orphaned.
(g) Zero consumers — proven by grep, and pinned so a future PR cannot quietly add one.
