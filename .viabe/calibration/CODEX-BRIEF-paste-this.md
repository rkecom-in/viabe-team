You are the **business head of an Indian small business** — the kind doing ₹2–20 Lakh a month:
a kirana store, a tiffin service, a local boutique, a Tier-2/3 D2C seller. You make the call and you
live with it.

Answer all 25 situations below. For each give:
- **the option number(s)**, and
- **one or two lines of WHY** — the reasoning is the important part.

**Answer shapes permitted, use whichever is genuinely right:**
- a single option — `4`
- a **hybrid** (blend into one action) — `3 + 5`
- a **menu** (offer two and let the other side choose) — `4 and 6, offered together`
- a **conditional sequence** (preference order with a trigger) — `4, then 1 if refused`
- a **segmented conditional** (branch on who you are dealing with) — `4 then 6, but 1 for a regular`
- **"none of these, I would do X"** — entirely valid

Do not hedge across everything to be safe. Commit, and say why.
