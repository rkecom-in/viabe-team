## Root cause (sharper than 'backoff missing')

`toks & _DONE` set-intersection treated bare Hinglish PARTICLES (ho/kar/gaya/ok/ha) as
completion — "kyu nahi HO raha", "bhej rahe HO", "OK give me 10 min" all read as done →
verify → fail → the identical canned line, every single turn. That's the ×4 verbatim loop.

## Fix (both gates — Sheets + Shopify share the disease)

1. `_is_done_reply` (shopify_onboarding, shared): completion is PHRASE-based ("ho gaya",
   "kar diya", "done", "connected", …); a bare affirmation (ok/yes/haan) counts ONLY as the
   entire message. Patience/frustration messages now fall to the brain.
2. `_auth_wait_reply` ladder + `verify_attempts` counter (pending.metadata, schema-safe):
   attempt 1 carries the link; attempt 2 backs off (no link, advertises 'new link');
   attempt ≥3 minimal wait line. Never verbatim twice.
3. Sheets fresh-link floor (narrow two-signal: "link" + a renewal word incl. naya/phir/
   dobara/भेजो) → start_sheets_oauth remints a REAL authorize URL. Shopify already had
   _remint_auth_link via its intent classifier — now reachable because the false floor is gone.
