## Defect (run 7)

The Manager asked "We found you're a Local services (repair etc.) business — is that right?"
— the generic TAXONOMY label for the internal key 'services' — moments before the profile
card presented the website's own wording ("AI-powered business intelligence…"). Two framings
of the same fact = a confused-looking Manager; and GST/HSN registrations are often wrong
(this GST says Warehouse/Depot).

## Fix

compose_onboarding_questions: when the draft carries a WEBSITE-sourced `category`/`about`
(provenance.source == 'website'), the business-type taxonomy confirm is SUPPRESSED — the
card presents the website's wording and the owner corrects by talking. The reconciled
taxonomy key still promotes internally (backend routing unchanged); only the contradictory
question dies. GBP/GST-only discoveries still confirm as before. (Internal reconcile already
prefers domain-derived signals — this was presentation.)

## Evidence

2 new pins (website-sourced → suppressed; gbp-sourced → retained); onboarding sweep 501
green; ruff clean.
